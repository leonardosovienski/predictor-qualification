# Funding

This is a research programme, not a product. Nothing here promises returns, edge, safety or generalisation. No funding, credits, incubation or institutional access has been received to date; nothing below is money in hand.

Each need follows the same chain: **current evidence → current limitation → resource constraint → funded milestone → measurable output**.

| # | Current evidence | Current limitation | Resource constraint | Funded milestone | Measurable output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Agent containment built and tested (six attestations; 58/58 joint test) | The scientific question (evidence vs authority) is untested | researcher time; compute for episodes; an independent human judge; held-out corpus construction | Experiment E1 pre-registered publicly, executed, reported | pre-registration record; report with pre-declared metrics; raw logs hashed |
| F2 | Historical point-in-time evaluation declared not viable for lack of timestamps | Two research lines blocked on data | money for licensed point-in-time market data | Decision on the historical arm of E2 and on E3 | a dated decision with evidence either way |
| F3 | Internal adversarial audits and qualification performed by agents | No independent human review | access to reviewers (institutional or paid) | External review of attestations and protocol | signed review report; list of findings |
| F4 | Several qualification gates blocked on a secondary environment and the owner's time | Attestations cannot be re-issued | compute/infrastructure (secondary environments); time | Gates closed; attestations re-issued | new attestation hashes; zero gates in blocked state |
| F5 | Prospective collections running slowly (E2, E4) | Insufficient statistical power | time; small data costs | Pre-declared sample sizes reached | checkpoint reports at pre-declared sizes |

## Kinds of resource

- **Money:** licensed data (F2), reviewer fees if institutional access is unavailable (F3), small infrastructure (F4).
- **Compute and infrastructure:** episode runs (F1), secondary environments (F4).
- **Institutional access and mentorship:** independent judges and reviewers (F1, F3). Access is not counted as funding received.
- **Time:** the binding constraint across F1, F4 and F5.

## Why trust this

The programme publishes what did not work: retractions, a contaminated baseline, an entire architecture removed, attestations that stopped re-validating, a seal left broken by decision. Hashes, counts, dates and limitations are verifiable without trusting private code.
